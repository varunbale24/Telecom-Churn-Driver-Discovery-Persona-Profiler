Top Churn Drivers
  1. Contract Type: Month-to-month contracts have the highest churn risk (42% churn rate)
  2. Tenure: Customers with less than 12 months tenure are 8x more likely to churn
  3. Monthly Charges: Customers paying >₹70/month have higher churn rates
  4. Internet Service: Fiber optic users churn more than DSL users (25% vs 12%)
  5. Tech Support: Lack of tech support is a significant risk factor

Decision Tree Rules
IF Contract = Month-to-month
    AND Tenure < 12 months
    AND Monthly Charges > ₹70
    AND Tech Support = No
THEN Risk = VERY HIGH (>80% probability)

IF Contract = Month-to-month
    AND Internet Service = Fiber optic
    AND Total Charges < ₹500
THEN Risk = HIGH (>60% probability)

IF Contract = One year
    AND Tenure > 6 months
THEN Risk = MEDIUM (20-40% probability)

IF Contract = Two year
    AND Tech Support = Yes
THEN Risk = LOW (<15% probability)
Recommended Actions
For High-Risk Customers:
  • Offer contract upgrades (1 or 2 year plans)
  • Bundle tech support and internet services
  • Provide loyalty discounts for tenure > 6 months
  • Proactive customer success calls
For All Customers:
  • Regular service quality monitoring
  • Transparent billing and communication
  • Cross-sell complementary services
  • Customer satisfaction surveys
